Traditional Argentinian Empanada Step by Step

What are Empanadas?

Empanadas are traditional Argentinian meal served at every home, restaurant or "parrilla" (argentinian steakhouse) with different kind of fillings. The most popular one is with minced beef filling.

Let's make some together!

Ingredients

  • 12 empanada doug
  • minced beef 1kg
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1/2 half of bell pepper
  • 1 tomato and 2 tablespoons of tomato puree
  • paprika
  • salt
  • cumin
  • (optional) chopped olives

Steps

  1. In a pot with hot oil , add the onion and bell pepper. Let them brown for a few minutes, and when they’re halfway cooked (the onion is translucent), add the garlic and a little salt and pepper.
  2. Turn up the heat and add all the ground beef at once. Stir the meat so it doesn’t stick. When the meat is seared, add the diced tomatoes and 2 tablespoons of tomato puree.
  3. Season with paprika and cumin, a little more salt and pepper Cover (not completely—leave a small opening) and let cook for about half an hour, stirring occasionally.
  4. Remove from the heat and let cool in the pot. Add the chopped olives and mix well.
  5. Divide the filling among the empanada wrappers, and seal with a traditional crimp.
  6. Place the meat empanadas on a baking sheet and bake in a hot oven until golden brown. Enjoy!
