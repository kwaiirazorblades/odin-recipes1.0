Traditional Argentinian Milanesa Step by Step

What are Milanesas?

Milanesas are basically thin slices of meat (usually beef or chicken) dipped in beaten eggs and coated with breadcrumbs, fried or baked. They are one of the most popular and traditional comfort foods in Argentina. They are typically served with French fries or smashed potatoes.

Let's prepare some!

Ingredients:

  • top round or eye of round
  • eggs
  • parsley
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • breadcrumbs
  • salt
  • pepper
  • eggs

Steps:

  1. Season the meat sprinkling some salt and black pepper. Followed by chopped parsley and garlic on each piece of the meat.
  2. Coat in egg. Whisk the eggs with some salt in a depp plate. Place a piece of meat inside and then flip, making sure all the surface is covered with eggs.
  3. Coat with breadcrumbs. Pour the breadcrumbs into a deep plate. Coat the meat with the breadcrumbs, pressing as you go.
  4. Stack the breaded steak on a plate while continuing with the rest.
  5. Bake and broil in a preheated oven for 6-10 mins and flipping them halfway through.
  6. Remove them from the oven and place them on a plate or cooling rack. Enjoy!
